# Interview the TOR

Covers the work, the problem, objectives and outcomes, and scope (IS/OS). Ask through `ask_question`. Record each answer as `TEL-nnn`. Stop when every required field is `confirmed`.

## Contract fields

- field: project id | question: What identifier should this work use?
- field: project name | question: What name should people use for this work?
- field: customer contact | question: Who can the team reach, by role and organisation only?
- field: owner | question: Who owns the document?
- field: stakeholders | question: Who else takes part?
- field: purpose | question: What outcome should a person be able to review?
- field: TOR scope summary | question: What does this work include, in one summary?

## Sections

- section: 1.1 | question: What identifies the document?
- section: 2.1 | question: What problem prompted the work?
- section: 2.2 | question: Why is this work being done?
- section: 2.3 | question: What objectives and outcomes should the work reach?
- section: 3.1 | question: Which items are in scope?
- section: 3.2 | question: Which items are out of scope?
- section: 3.3 | question: What high-level requirements apply?
- section: 4.1 | question: What software will be delivered?
- section: 4.2 | question: What documents will be delivered?
- section: 4.3 | question: How will someone accept the result?
- section: 5.1 | question: What technical or organisational constraints apply?
- section: 5.2 | question: What timeline applies, or is no date set?
- section: 5.3 | question: Who is responsible, and what does the work depend on?
- section: 6 | question: Which references apply, or is the answer none?
